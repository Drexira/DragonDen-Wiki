About Dragon Den

I’m Drexira, a self-taught programmer who mostly works on mods, tools, and game-related projects.

I started by tinkering with tiny scripts and simple tweaks. Broke a lot of things. Fixed them. Learned a ton. Kept going.

I develop my own mods for SPT and Unity projects whenever an idea pops into my head.

Dragon Den is my twist on Tarkov. It is where cuteness, edgyness, and weirdness collide while staying believable.

  • • Traders with their own quirks and attitude
  • • Custom items and quests that fills in the empty space
  • • Backpacks, armor, facewear, headwear and attachments
  • • Odd items that should not exist but still fits right into Tarkov

No custom Weapons, just everything else that makes Tarkov less boring.

If you’re curious about my work, check the Dragon Den Mods section in the sidebar or use the search to jump straight to what you need.

Thanks for dropping by the Den.
